Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the "Basketball AI" mobile app (shown as "Match Analysis", "Análisis Fútbol", "Spielanalyse", "Analyse Foot", "Analisi Calcio" or "Análise Futebol" depending on your device language; the "App"), developed by Ciner Labs ("we", "us") and published on Google Play and the Apple App Store, handles user information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or phone number.
  • The App contains no advertising, ad tracking or analytics tools.
  • The App does not request device permissions such as location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • If you buy a Premium subscription, your purchase information is processed by our subscription provider RevenueCat to verify your subscription (details in section 3).
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data and do not share it with third parties for advertising.

2. Information the App uses

Game data: Game lists, probabilities, predictions, AI-written summaries and track record statistics shown in the App are downloaded from a public server (GitHub). Live scores during games come from a small relay server we operate (Cloudflare Workers); it only returns scores, asks for no information and keeps no request logs. No personal information is sent during this download. As with any internet connection, the server that answers the request may technically receive your device's IP address and device type. This information is not passed on to us and we do not process it. GitHub explains how it handles such information in its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Information stored on your device: Your confirmation that you are 18 or older and that you accept the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy (the accepted version and the time of acceptance) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. It is removed when you delete the App.

AI summaries: Match summaries are generated on our server from match probabilities, independently of you. No user information is used for, or sent to, AI services.

3. In-app purchases and subscriptions

When you buy a Premium subscription, payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never have access to your card or payment details.

To check whether your subscription is active, to restore purchases and to keep your subscription status consistent across your devices, we use the subscription service RevenueCat, Inc. ("RevenueCat"). For this purpose the following information is sent to RevenueCat:

  • A random user number created by the App, which does not reveal your identity
  • Purchase information: subscription plan, purchase and renewal dates, subscription status and the purchase record provided by the store
  • Technical information: operating system and app version, country/region and IP address during the connection

This information is used only to manage and verify your subscription. It is not used for advertising or profiling and is not sold. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf in line with its privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Payments are also subject to the privacy policy of the relevant store:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

If you do not buy a subscription, no purchase information is sent to RevenueCat.

4. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information stored on your device (age and terms confirmation) is removed when you delete the App. If you bought a subscription, your subscription records are kept at RevenueCat for as long as needed to manage your subscription and to meet legal retention obligations. You can ask for these records to be deleted by writing to the contact address below; we will act on your request within 30 days.

How to request deletion of your data

  1. Send an email with the subject "Data deletion request" to kaanciner@icloud.com.
  2. So that we can find your records, include the order number of your subscription (on Google Play the number starting with "GPA.", on the App Store the order number on Apple's receipt) or the purchase date. As the App has no accounts, we do not ask for any other identifying information.
  3. We will complete your request within 30 days and confirm by email.

Deleted: your anonymous user number at RevenueCat, your purchase and subscription history and related technical information.

Retained: we do not keep data ourselves; only the email exchange about your request is kept for 1 year so that we can show the request was handled. Payment records are kept by Google Play or Apple under their own policies and are outside our control.

6. Security

All connections between the App and the server are encrypted (HTTPS).

7. Your rights (EEA, UK and other regions)

Users who do not buy a subscription: no personal data is processed through the App.

Users who buy a subscription: the purchase information described in section 3 is processed to provide the subscription you purchased (performance of a contract, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). RevenueCat is located in the United States; data is transferred there under appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses included in RevenueCat's data processing terms.

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Users in Turkey also have the rights listed in Article 11 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Important information

The App does not offer gambling or betting services, does not handle deposits or withdrawals and does not link to third-party betting sites. Predictions in the App are statistical probabilities; no result is guaranteed and the content is for information and entertainment only.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates are published on this page and the "Last updated" date above is changed.
